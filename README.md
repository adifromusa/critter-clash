# Critter Clash

A fast, original creature card duel. Play solo against Rhea, or pass and play with a friend on the same device.

## Play

- Netlify: https://critter-clash.netlify.app
- GitHub Pages: https://adifromusa.github.io/critter-clash/
- Or open `index.html` in any modern browser.

## Features

- Solo mode with Easy, Normal, Hard, and Adaptive AI difficulty.
- Local two-player pass-and-play with a private hand-off screen between turns.
- Evolution lines: play a basic critter, then evolve it into a stronger form, up to a rare third form.
- Win solo battles to earn packs with one rare, three common cards, and two energy cards.
- Build a custom 24-card deck from your collection.
- Distinct attack visuals and sounds for Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, Rock, and Ice.
- One-card-at-a-time hand preview and pack reveal.
- Works on desktop, iPad, and phones. Your collection, deck, packs, and record are stored in your browser.
